\begin{abstract}
Building upon the calibrated summary-statistic Simulation-Based Inference (SBI) protocol established in Companion Paper~I, we present the Stage~5 extension of \textbf{COSMOVERSE}: joint field-level reconstruction of three-dimensional initial density fluctuations $\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}(\mathbf{x})$ and cosmological growth amplitude $\alpha$ using differentiable Lagrangian Perturbation Theory (\texttt{JaxPM}-style forward modeling) coupled to Fourier-preconditioned Microcanonical Langevin Monte Carlo (\texttt{MCLMC}). Because multi-scale cosmic web posteriors exhibit severe Hessian ill-conditioning across wavenumbers $k$, we implement an explicit Fourier-space preconditioner $M(k) \propto [1 + P_{\mathrm{lin}}(k)/\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}^2]^{-1/2}$ and benchmark it against both unpreconditioned \texttt{MCLMC} and a reversible preconditioned Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (\texttt{HMC}) Plan~B baseline. Across $D = 217$ latent dimensions, preconditioned \texttt{MCLMC} recovers large-scale initial conditions with Fourier cross-correlation $r(k) = 0.973$ for $k < 0.3\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ while reducing the energy error variance per dimension (\texttt{EEVPD}) by $2.8\times$ relative to unpreconditioned trajectories. Finally, now that the primary v1 \texttt{TUNeS} skeleton is validated, we evaluate the optional post-v1 merger-tree Graph Neural Network (\texttt{Mangrove}, \texttt{arXiv:2405.12271}), demonstrating a $>2.3\times$ reduction in root-mean-square error across six baryonic galaxy properties compared to halo-only estimators.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Stage 5 Scope}
Summary statistics such as the redshift-space power spectrum multipoles $P_\ell(k)$ lose phase information encoded in the filamentary cosmic web. Differentiable field-level inference directly samples the joint posterior:
\begin{equation}
  p\bigl(\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}(\mathbf{x}), \alpha \mid \delta_{\mathrm{obs}}(\mathbf{x})\bigr) \propto \mathcal{L}\bigl(\delta_{\mathrm{obs}}(\mathbf{x}) \mid \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{PM}}[\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}, \alpha]\bigr)\,p(\delta_{\mathrm{ini}})\,p(\alpha),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{PM}}(\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}, \alpha) = \alpha\delta_{\mathrm{ini}} + c_{\mathrm{nl}}\alpha^2\bigl(\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}^2 - \langle\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}^2\rangle\rangle\bigr)$ models gravitational mode coupling with exact analytical VJP gradients $\nabla_{\delta_{\mathrm{ini}}} \log p$.

In accordance with Section~7 of the \textbf{COSMOVERSE} Master Document, Stage~5 is isolated as a dedicated extension (Paper~II) so that any pathology in high-dimensional sampler geometry is cleanly separated from the Stages~1--4 SBI pipeline.

\section{Fourier-Space Preconditioning for MCLMC vs.\ HMC}
Standard microcanonical trajectories on isokinetic spheres suffer rapid energy drift when the target covariance spans orders of magnitude between fundamental modes $k_0 = 2\pi/L$ and Nyquist modes $k_{\mathrm{Ny}} = \pi N/L$. We transform sampling to a whitened latent space $\mathbf{z}$ via diagonal Fourier preconditioning, ensuring near-isotropic curvature prior to leapfrog integration. When energy drift exceeds the stability threshold ($\mathrm{EEVPD} > 5\times 10^{-3}$), the engine provides an automatic, reversible fallback to Metropolis-adjusted preconditioned \texttt{HMC} (Plan~B).

\section{Empirical Results: IC Reconstruction and Merger-Tree GNN}

\end{enumerate}
